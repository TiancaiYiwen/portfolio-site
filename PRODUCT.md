# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + one CSS file (`assets/css/site.css`), no build step, minimal/no JavaScript. Hosted on GitHub Pages at `tiancaiyiwen.github.io/portfolio-site/`. Relative paths only. Exported Articulate Storyline (`story.html`) and Rise (`index.html`) courses live under `/courses/<project>/`.

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and recruiters at **corporate L&D teams** and **EdTech / AI learning companies** screening candidates for instructional design and learning experience design **internships**. They skim fast, often between many portfolios, and decide in under a minute whether to open a case study or launch a project.

Secondary: Yiwen and her husband, who maintain the site by editing HTML directly.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio of **Yiwen Li**, a Ph.D. student in Learning, Literacies and Technology at Arizona State University (expected May 2028), seeking an ID/LXD internship while finishing her Ph.D. Success = a reviewer opens a case study, launches a project, and reaches out by email.

## Positioning

She **designs learning and measures whether it works.** Most entry-level ID candidates can build a course; few can design the evaluation, validate a survey instrument, or analyze learner logs. Her evidence: co-designed two LLM-powered Socratic tutors compared in a university biology course; developed and validated a 23-item survey scale with 1,000+ students; taught task-based lessons to adult learners.

## Operating Context

Reviewers arrive from a résumé link, LinkedIn, or a job application. They scan the home page, open the featured project, skim case-study sections (problem, objectives, design decisions, evaluation plan), and may launch the Storyline/Rise course in the browser. Many view on laptops; some on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Work, three case studies (shared template), About, Résumé, Contact.
- Three planned projects: flagship Storyline 360 branching simulation, Rise 360 course, microlearning/job aid. Not built yet; placeholders `[[...]]` mark missing content.
- Projects are **Independent Portfolio Projects** (concept work), labeled as such.
- No backend, no forms; contact via email link (`yiwenli2@asu.edu`) and LinkedIn.
- Large videos embedded from an external host, not stored in the repo.

## Brand Commitments

- Name is always "Yiwen Li" (never "Dora").
- Title: "Learning Designer & Researcher".
- Honest, plain, human voice. No generic AI marketing language, no keyword stuffing.

## Evidence on Hand

- Résumé facts (education, research, teaching, publications) on `resume/index.html` and `about/index.html`.
- Casual photo: `assets/img/yiwen.jpg` (used on About).
- **Absent, must not be fabricated:** employers/clients for portfolio projects, learner data, outcome statistics, testimonials, Storyline/Rise proficiency claims beyond "currently learning".

## Product Principles

1. Reasoning over decoration: the case studies' thinking is the product.
2. Lead with the design-plus-evaluation edge.
3. Honest framing of concept work; never inflate.
4. Fast to scan: a reviewer should find the flagship project and contact in seconds.
5. Easy for non-specialists to edit later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic landmarks, skip link, keyboard navigation, visible focus, AA contrast, alt text, reduced-motion support, no autoplay. Accessibility is also a hiring signal for ID roles.
